        # Characters to add:

        Add characters: cucumber, squeak, squeal, screech, creak, chirp, peep, yelp, grate, whine, screak, pipe.

        1. Squeak (Short, high-pitched, often metallic or plastic)

        A rusty door hinge: "The old door squeaked on its hinges as I opened it".
        Sneakers on a polished floor: "His sneakers squeaked on the gym floor".
        A mouse or small toy: "The rubber toy squeaked when the dog bit it".
        Windshield wipers on dry glass: "The dry wipers squeaked across the windshield".

        2. Squeal (Longer, higher-pitched, often harsh)

        Tires breaking suddenly: "The car's tires squealed as the driver slammed on the brakes".
        A child in excitement: "The children squealed with delight at the fireworks".
        A loose car belt: "The engine emitted a loud squeal upon starting".
        A pig or distressed animal: "The piglets squealed in the pen".

        3. Screech (High-pitched, harsh, harsh piercing sound)

        Brakes: "The train made a loud screeching sound when it stopped".
        Nocturnal animals: "The barn owl emitted a rasping, harsh screech".
        Nails on a blackboard: "The sound of her nails on the board made everyone wince".
        A panicked scream: "She let out a piercing screech when she saw the spider".

        4. Creak (Long, low-pitched, dry sound)

        Wooden floorboards: "The floorboards creaked under his weight".
        An old chair: "The rocking chair creaked back and forth".
        Wooden stairs: "I heard the creak of the wooden stairs at night".

        5. Chirp (Short, high-pitched, melodic)

        Birds/Birdsong: "I woke up to the sound of birds chirping".
        Crickets: "The crickets kept chirping all night".
        Electronic devices: "The smoke alarm gave a low chirp when the battery was low".

        6. Peep (Small, quiet, high sound)

        Baby birds: "The chicks peeped for food".
        A small horn: "The toy horn gave a tiny peep".

        7. Yelp (Short, sharp, high-pitched cry)

        A dog in pain: "The puppy gave a yelp when I stepped on its tail".
        Sudden surprise: "She gave a quick yelp of surprise".

        8. Grate (Harsh, rasping sound)

        Metal rubbing: "The broken gear grated against the housing".
        Teeth: "He grated his teeth together in frustration".

        9. Whine (High-pitched, long, complaining sound)

        An engine/motor: "The electric motor began to whine as it accelerated".
        A pet complaining: "The dog whined at the door to be let in".
        Wind: "The wind whistled and whined through the trees".

        10. Screak (A grating or screeching sound)

        A rusty gate: "The old iron gate gave a harsh screak as it was forced open".

        11. Pipe (A high-pitched musical sound)

        A bird's call: "The curlews piped in the marsh".
        Wind through a narrow gap: "The wind piped loudly through the keyhole".
