# About the Mod

The mod modifies the kitten model and adds a mouse in place of the kitten head.
The idea is that a mouse can control the astronaut suit via brain implants.

# The Lore story

Space is dangerous. The risk of losing a cute kitten astronaut is unbearable.

So the kittens started a new program to reduce that risk. The program was called "Mouse the Cyborg."
Its purpose was to use mice as expendable astronauts. A mouse was fitted with a brain implant in its helmet.
The spacesuit was modified and built as a robot-spaceship without a head. The mouse in the helmet had full control of the spacesuit.

Many tests were conducted, but one day a spacesuit with a mouse was not found.
It happened after the kittens brought the mice to watch a movie called "Space Cucumbers: The Invaders."
The mice observed the kittens and saw that they were experiencing fear and horror...

Who knows what the mice were thinking at that moment? Maybe it was some kind of revenge?

The kittens started a search mission. They decided to investigate nearby islands.
A rocket carrying a kitten named Maxi was launched toward the possible location of the lost spacesuit.
But a malfunctioning computer messed up the navigation, and the rocket crash-landed on an unknown island.

Eventually, Maxi's collar was found on the beach. The kittens launched a rescue mission to the island.
Maxi was found in a rocket full of cucumbers.

After this accident, a mysterious new company began offering contracts to deliver cucumbers to space.
In reality, the mice had created the company to deliver cucumbers to space and advance the "Cucumber Project".
The mice hacked rockets or offered contracts to send cucumbers to various locations.

The mice were probably searching for a home planet for cucumbers, one that was suitable for them to grow
and mutate into cucumber-like alien creatures.

The packets from the mysterious company ("Infinite Cosmos Cucumber Logistics Solutions," or ICCLS)
were quite large and looked like refrigerators with several sealed doors. Labels reading "Do not open!"
were glued onto every packet door.
However, the space missions for this company usually ended in mysterious accidents,
such as navigation loss, leaks, strange sounds, cut wires, flying loose cucumbers, and drilled holes.
Only blind kittens (or kittens wearing protective eye covers) could transport cucumbers in rockets without issue.

The Flight Safety Agency demanded an investigation into the accidents.
Suspicion was raised during the cargo bay audit. In addition to cucumbers,
almost frozen and sleeping mice were found in the ICCLS company’s packets.

The kittens started a secret investigation into ICCLS activity.

1. Squeak (Short, high-pitched, often metallic or plastic)

    A rusty door hinge: "The old door squeaked on its hinges as I opened it".
    Sneakers on a polished floor: "His sneakers squeaked on the gym floor".
    A mouse or small toy: "The rubber toy squeaked when the dog bit it".
    Windshield wipers on dry glass: "The dry wipers squeaked across the windshield".

2. Squeal (Longer, higher-pitched, often harsh)

    Tires breaking suddenly: "The car's tires squealed as the driver slammed on the brakes".
    A child in excitement: "The children squealed with delight at the fireworks".
    A loose car belt: "The engine emitted a loud squeal upon starting".
    A pig or distressed animal: "The piglets squealed in the pen".

3. Screech (High-pitched, harsh, harsh piercing sound)

    Brakes: "The train made a loud screeching sound when it stopped".
    Nocturnal animals: "The barn owl emitted a rasping, harsh screech".
    Nails on a blackboard: "The sound of her nails on the board made everyone wince".
    A panicked scream: "She let out a piercing screech when she saw the spider".

4. Creak (Long, low-pitched, dry sound)

    Wooden floorboards: "The floorboards creaked under his weight".
    An old chair: "The rocking chair creaked back and forth".
    Wooden stairs: "I heard the creak of the wooden stairs at night".

5. Chirp (Short, high-pitched, melodic)

    Birds/Birdsong: "I woke up to the sound of birds chirping".
    Crickets: "The crickets kept chirping all night".
    Electronic devices: "The smoke alarm gave a low chirp when the battery was low".

6. Peep (Small, quiet, high sound)

    Baby birds: "The chicks peeped for food".
    A small horn: "The toy horn gave a tiny peep".

7. Yelp (Short, sharp, high-pitched cry)

    A dog in pain: "The puppy gave a yelp when I stepped on its tail".
    Sudden surprise: "She gave a quick yelp of surprise".

8. Grate (Harsh, rasping sound)

    Metal rubbing: "The broken gear grated against the housing".
    Teeth: "He grated his teeth together in frustration".

9. Whine (High-pitched, long, complaining sound)

    An engine/motor: "The electric motor began to whine as it accelerated".
    A pet complaining: "The dog whined at the door to be let in".
    Wind: "The wind whistled and whined through the trees".

10. Screak (A grating or screeching sound)

    A rusty gate: "The old iron gate gave a harsh screak as it was forced open".

11. Pipe (A high-pitched musical sound)

    A bird's call: "The curlews piped in the marsh".
    Wind through a narrow gap: "The wind piped loudly through the keyhole".
